Primary and secondary orientations combine into six distinct profiles.

Each person's primary orientation defines how they draw energy and where they add the most natural value. Their secondary shapes how that value is expressed — producing six profiles, each with a distinct operating signature that leaders can identify, position, and build around.

Three
Brains Framework
HOW – WHAT
Precision-driven, progress-oriented

They see how systems will break before they break, and build things that hold.

"This works for now, but we need a real process here or it's going to break at scale."

HOW – WHY
Precision-driven, purpose-oriented

They understand how things work so deeply that they always find a better way.

"Wait — before we move on, what happens when X breaks? Because it will."

WHAT – HOW
Progress-driven, precision-oriented

They move with urgency, course-correct in real time, and refuse to stand still.

"Before we leave this room, I need owners and a deadline on each of these."

WHAT – WHY
Progress-driven, purpose-oriented

They move toward goals with conviction — fast, direct, and without waiting for perfect information.

"We've been talking about this for 45 minutes. Can we just pick something and move?"

WHY – HOW
Purpose-driven, precision-oriented

They hold the deepest question and the finest detail simultaneously, and won't let go of either.

"I want to understand the whole picture before we commit to this direction."

WHY – WHAT
Purpose-driven, progress-oriented

They can't commit to a direction they don't believe in — and once they do, they move.

"Before we go further — are we solving the right problem here?"
