The MindPrint Framework

Three lenses.
One powerful system.

At the heart of Curio's approach is a deceptively simple insight: every person naturally sees the world through one of three lenses — Why, What, or How. These aren't fixed personality types. They are dynamic ways of approaching problems.

Understanding which lens each team member leads with — and how those lenses interact — is the key to unlocking extraordinary team performance.

01
Lens One
Why

Purpose-driven thinkers who lead with vision, meaning, and the bigger picture. They ask: What is this for? Does it matter? Where are we going? They energize teams with direction and inspire belief in what's possible.

VisionPurposeStrategyInspiration
02
Lens Two
What

Analytical thinkers who lead with data, structure, and clarity. They ask: What do we know? What does the evidence say? What exactly needs to happen? They bring rigor and precision to every challenge.

AnalysisStructureDataClarity
03
Lens Three
How

Action-oriented thinkers who lead with execution, process, and practicality. They ask: How do we do this? What's the next step? They transform ideas into reality and keep teams moving forward.

ExecutionProcessActionResults
The Energy Model

Where your energy goes shapes everything

Every person has a primary lens that energizes them, a secondary lens that's neutral, and a tertiary lens that actively drains them when overused.

When leaders understand their team's energy dynamics, they can structure work in ways that fuel people rather than deplete them — and the result is a team that does more and feels better doing it.

This isn't about labeling people. It's about recognizing that the right work, matched to the right mind, unlocks performance that no mandate ever could.

Our Approach

A three-phase path to transformation

We don't just deliver insights and walk away. Curio partners with you through a structured engagement designed to create lasting change in how your team thinks, communicates, and collaborates.

01
Discovery
Learning About Your Team

Before we can help your team evolve, we need to understand it deeply. We gather rich data about how each person naturally approaches problems and how your team's work is currently structured.

  • Individual MindPrint assessment for each team member
  • Leadership questionnaire on team dynamics and work types
  • Identification of energy patterns and misalignments
  • Analysis of the problem types your team regularly faces
02
Alignment
Establishing a Common Language

We bring the team together in a transformative 2-day workshop that gives everyone a shared framework for understanding how they — and their colleagues — think. What was invisible becomes visible and actionable.

  • Immersive 2-day team workshop
  • Problem-solving theory and typology
  • The six thinking profiles revealed
  • The Energy Model — understanding your team's fuel
  • Strategies for working across thinking styles
  • Team purpose and optimization planning
03
Optimization
A New Way to Look at Work

We work closely with leadership to translate insights into actionable strategy — customized specifically to your team's composition and the challenges you face.
